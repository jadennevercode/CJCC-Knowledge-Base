## Universal Flash Storage (UFS) 
UFS 5.0 is the latest embedded flash storage standard designed to meet the demanding requirements of nextgeneration mobile devices,such as premium smartphones with advanced on-device Al features. Building on the MIPI M-PHY version 6.0 physical layer and UniPro' version 3.0 protoco, UFS 5.0 introduces the High-Speed-Gear6,supporting theoreticalinterace speeds up to 46.6 Gb/s per lane and approximately 10.8 GB/s of real-world read/write performance using dual lanes'.
