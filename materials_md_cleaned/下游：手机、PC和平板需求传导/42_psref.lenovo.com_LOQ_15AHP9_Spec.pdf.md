# OPERATING REQUIREMENTS

## Operating Environment

**Temperature[1]**

[Source Page: 7]

- At altitudes up to 2438 m (8,000 ft)
  - Operating: 5°C to 35°C (41°F to 95°F)
  - Storage: 5°C to 43°C (41°F to 109°F)
- At altitudes above 2438 m (8,000 ft)
  - Maximum temperature when operating under the unpressurized condition: 31.3°C (88°F)

**Relative Humidity**

- Operating: 8% to 95% at wet-bulb temperature 23°C (73°F)
- Storage: 5% to 95% at wet-bulb temperature 27°C (81°F)

**Altitude:** Maximum altitude (without pressurization): 3048 m (10,000 ft)

Notes: [1] When you charge the battery, its temperature must be no lower than 10°C (50°F).
