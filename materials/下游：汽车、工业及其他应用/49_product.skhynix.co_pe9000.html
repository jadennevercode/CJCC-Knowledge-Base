PE9000 series

Best-in-class performance

for Datacenters

and enterprise Servers

product detail

SK hynix’s Second Gen4 SSDs with Cutting-Edge NAND Flash
Following the PE9000 lineup, the PCIe 4.0-enabled PE9000 series is SK hynix’s second in-house ASIC that comes in various form factors
including E1.S, M.2 and U.2.
The PE9000 series offers a lineup of PE9110 integrating 6th-gen 4D V-NAND and PE9010 with the latest 7th-gen 4D V-NAND.
Best-in-Class Performance & Power Efficiency
The PE9000 series delivers industry-leading power efficiency driven by the latest 7th-gen TLC V-NAND and enhanced firmware. The E1.S form factor supports capacities up to 2 to 8TB, while M.2 1 to 4TB.
NVMe SSDs Optimized for Enterprise Workload
PE9110 with E1.S form factor is optimized to manage enterprise workloads. Sequential write speed is critical for enterprises when managing data in big chunks. With SW speed of 6300MB/s, PE9110 offers up to 95% higher performance than the prior generation.
E1.S the New Key Form Factor for Datacenters
PE9000 are available in the cutting-edge E1.s form factor. U.2 and M.2 used to constitute a majority of the form factors in the past. E1.S however, has recently fast-replaced these two form factors as it boasts lower power draw against U.2 and is hot-pluggable unlike M.2. Going forward, E1.S will continue to undergo exceptional growth. The PE9000 series comes in the E1.S form factor delivering unrivaled power efficiency.
