    About us

    Full Stack AI Memory Creator

    Pioneering Memory
    Innovation in the AI Era

    With our global technology leadership, SK hynix strives to deliver greater value to all stakeholders, including customers, partners, investors, local communities, and employees.
    We are strengthening our ESG management to create more social value and ensure sustainable growth, moving beyond a business model focused solely on economic benefits.

    As the AI era accelerates, SK hynix is evolving from a memory supplier into a “Full Stack AI Memory Creator.”
    This represents our commitment to go beyond meeting customer demands — to become a co-designer, co-architect, and ecosystem partner that drives breakthroughs in AI computing together with our customers.
    By integrating advanced DRAM and NAND technologies, SK hynix will continue to build the core value of the AI era through its full-stack AI memory innovations.

    Seeking continuous business growth with DRAM and NAND

    SK hynix is a global leader in the memory semiconductor industry, driving the digital era forward with innovative memory solutions.

    New WindowProduct Site

    DRAM

    SK hynix has secured the industry’s top-level micro process technology based on constant R&D and is constantly developing premium products that use less power than existing products but also have significantly improved capacity, speed, and performance.
    By launching the world’s first DDR5 and expanding our market dominance of strategic products including HBM, SK hynix will further solidify its technological leadership that leads the industry.

    NAND Flash

    Since introducing the world’s first CTF-based 4D NAND Flash (96-layer 512Gb TLC) in 2018, SK hynix has maintained technological leadership in the flash memory sector with milestones such as the development of the world’s first 238-layer 4D NAND and the mass production of 321-layer 4D NAND.
    In the era of AI, SK hynix will continue to deliver optimized solutions to support the reliable operation of massive data.
