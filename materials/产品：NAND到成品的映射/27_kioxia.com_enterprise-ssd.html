Enterprise SSD

Enterprise SSDs are suitable for high-performance computing, server and storage systems that require high levels of performance and reliability.

KIOXIA Enterprise SSDs equip the flash memory and controller developed by KIOXIA and offer high reliability, data protection incorporating power-loss-protection (PLP) and encryption technology to support enterprise environments and applications. Light weight and low power consumption will make the systems more energy efficient.

KIOXIA CM Series

KIOXIA CM Series SSDs are High-Performance PCIe® / NVMe™ SSDs available in E1.S, E3.S and 2.5-inch form factors. PLP feature is available on these SSD to safeguard enterprise-level critical data. These enterprise SSDs have the model supporting direct liquid cooling and deliver stable performance with efficient heat management in high density environments. Customer can select dual ports option and a range of security/encryption options.

  • Definition of capacity: KIOXIA Corporation defines a megabyte (MB) as 1,000,000 bytes, a gigabyte (GB) as 1,000,000,000 bytes and a terabyte (TB) as 1,000,000,000,000 bytes.  A computer operating system, however, reports storage capacity using powers of 2 for the definition of 1GB = 230 = 1,073,741,824 bytes and therefore shows less storage capacity.  Available storage capacity (including examples of various media files) will vary based on file size, formatting, settings, software and operating system, such as Microsoft Operating System and/or pre-installed software applications, or media content.  Actual formatted capacity may vary.
  • DWPD: Drive Write Per Day. One full drive write per day means the drive can be written and re-written to full capacity once a day every day for five years, the stated product warranty period. Actual results may vary due to system configuration, usage and other factors.
  • Read and write speed may vary depending on the host device, read and write conditions, and file size.
