Response to the earthquake that occurred on April 20 in Tohoku, Japan

  • April 21, 2026
  • Kioxia Holdings Corporation

Regarding the earthquake that occurred in Tohoku region around 16:53 on April 20, the situation at the Kitakami Plant which is in Tohoku region is as follows:

The company confirmed that the constructions and facilities of buildings sustained no damage at the Kitakami Plant. The Kitakami Plant is continuing its production activities as usual.
