## Micron UFS 4.1 for next-gen automotive

Micron UFS 4.1 for automotive is the world’s first UFS 4.1 automotive solution built with G9 NAND, setting a new industry standard for intelligent vehicle storage. Engineered for advanced AI-driven systems, it delivers outstanding speed, reliability, and safety, with ASIL-B compliance, ASPICE certification, robust cybersecurity, and enhanced thermal protection.

## G9 NAND in the Micron 2650 SSD

The first SSD to ship with the industry's 9th-generation 3D NAND technology, Micron G9 NAND, the Micron 2650 NVMe™ SSD provides best-in-class everyday computing user experience and performance that beats competitive client SSDs.
