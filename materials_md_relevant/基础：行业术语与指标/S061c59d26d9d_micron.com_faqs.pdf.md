# Customer Service Note

# Wafer-Level Packaging and Packaging Materials Composition

## Introduction

Whole wafers of Micron’s DRAM, NAND Flash memory, and NOR are packaged according to specific procedures to help prevent damage during shipping. Micron uses two methods for packaging wafers, including horizontal wafer shippers and vendor boxes. Micron’s wafer shipments also include various labels on the inner and outer packages to enable easy identification of contents and verification of orders. Packaging Materials Composition on page 12 provides complete shipping and recycling information about each of the materials used for shipping Micron® wafers.

[Source Page: 3]

## Storage Requirements

Micron wafer products are packaged in a controlled environment for shipping. Upon receipt, the customer should transfer the wafers to a similar environment for storage. Micron recommends the wafers be maintained in a filtered nitrogen atmosphere until removed for assembly. The recommended moisture content of the storage facility should be maintained at room temperature and relative humidity, and the product should be stored in the original moisture barrier bag packaging, which includes desiccant. Electrostatic discharge (ESD) damage precautions are necessary during handling. The wafer must be in an ESD-protected environment at all times for inspection and assembly.

Under these conditions, customers can keep wafer products contained in horizontal wafer shippers or vendor boxes in storage for up to six months.
