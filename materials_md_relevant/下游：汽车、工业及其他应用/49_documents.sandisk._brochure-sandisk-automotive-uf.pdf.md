## Fast Boot

iNAND® automotive-grade e.MMC and UFS devices enable the host to boot from boot partitions/LUs of the device.

This feature allows the system to be functional at the proper time. For example, the console display and the remaining car system functionality are needed the moment the driver enters or activates the vehicle. In addition, this feature enables the replacement of SPI NOR for the inherent capability of the iNAND® device, reducing BOM costs and board space.
